This site gets a new palette every so often. Each edition gets a short note like this one, with a style tile at the top: one board showing the colours, the type pairing and how the core elements look together. The tile is built from that edition's saved values, so it keeps showing this palette long after the site has moved on to the next one.

Edition 01 is the baseline the site was built on.

## The palette

**Warm paper** (#F7F6F2) instead of pure white. It reads more like a page than a screen and is easier on the eyes over a long read.

**Near-black ink** (#141414) for text and the heavy rules above cards. Softer than pure black on warm paper, with plenty of contrast.

**Muted terminal green** (#2E6F5C) as the single accent, for links, labels and callouts. It nods to the command line without shouting.

The lighter tones, like secondary text, dates and the hairline dividers, are mixed from ink and paper rather than picked separately. That keeps every grey on the page related, and every text colour is checked against WCAG AA contrast before the site builds.

## The type

**Inter** carries headings and body copy. It's neutral and very legible at small sizes.

**JetBrains Mono** handles the structural layer: file-path labels like `~/field-notes`, dates and phase markers. It's what gives the site its terminal feel.

## The structure

The layout follows Swiss grid discipline: generous whitespace, hairline rules, and a single vertical rule beside the hero as the signature gesture. A faint vignette softens the edges of the screen, a nod to paper and e-readers that costs nothing in page weight.

Next edition, the colours change and the structure stays.
