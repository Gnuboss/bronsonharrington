Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Fractional Growth Leadership.

Answers to the questions most clients ask before starting a fractional engagement.

How is fractional growth leadership different from hiring an SEO consultant?

A consultant advises. A fractional growth leader owns the strategy and is accountable for outcomes. The difference shows up in how involved they are in your team's day-to-day work, your stakeholder reporting, and the decisions that shape your organic growth program.

How many hours per month does a fractional engagement involve?

It varies by scope. Strategic advisory engagements typically run eight to twelve hours per month. Full fractional leadership engagements run higher depending on team size, reporting requirements, and the complexity of the growth program.

Can you work with our existing agency?

Yes. Fractional leadership often sits above an agency relationship, providing the senior client-side counterpart that holds the agency accountable and ensures their work serves the business strategy rather than just the agency's deliverable list.

How long is the minimum engagement?

Three months is the minimum for a fractional leadership engagement. Strategic advisory can be month-to-month. The first three months establish the strategy, team relationships, and reporting cadence that make the engagement productive.

Do you work with businesses outside iGaming?

Yes. While iGaming and regulated verticals are the deepest specialism, the fractional leadership model applies across any business that needs senior organic growth strategy without a full-time hire.

How do we measure success?

Success metrics are agreed at the start of every engagement and tied to business outcomes - organic revenue, qualified lead volume, market share, or specific ranking and visibility targets depending on your growth stage and objectives.
