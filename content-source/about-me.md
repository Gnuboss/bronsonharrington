My Story

<blockquote>
<p>My first website was so poorly optimized it wouldn’t fit on a 1.44MB floppy disk.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>That mistake taught me something important.</p>
<p>Optimization isn’t something you do at the end.</p>
<p>It’s something you build into everything from the beginning.</p>
<p>Long before search engines, smartphones, social media, or artificial intelligence, I was the kid spending hours in front of a computer connected to a cassette tape drive. I learned BASIC from books thicker than the computer’s manual, experimented endlessly, and discovered that technology wasn’t just something you used—it was something you could create with.</p>
<p>One of my earliest adventures involved teaching myself graphic design on a 486 PC running Photoshop in 256 colours. Back then, every pixel mattered. Every kilobyte mattered. Every limitation forced creativity.</p>
<p>That curiosity eventually led me into graphic design, web design, webmastering, SEO, analytics, and growth strategy.</p>
<p>I learned the web during the era of hand-coded HTML, PHP includes, CSS, Joomla, and early WordPress. I watched search engines transform how people discovered information. I witnessed the rise of mobile, social media, cloud computing, and now artificial intelligence.</p>
<p>The tools have changed dramatically.</p>
<p>The mindset hasn’t.</p>
<p>I’ve always been driven by the same questions:</p>
<ul>
<li><em>How does this work?</em></li>
<li><em>How can it be improved?</em></li>
<li><em>How can it create more value for the people using it?</em></li>
</ul>
