When You Need This

Signs it's Time for Senior SEO Consulting.

If any of these situations sound familiar, a senior strategist's perspective is probably overdue.

Your organic traffic dropped after a core update and hasn't recovered

Algorithm updates hit regulated and YMYL verticals differently from standard niches. Recovery requires understanding exactly which quality signals Google adjusted and how your site was exposed. Generic recovery advice rarely works here.

You're scaling a white-label or multi-property portfolio

Managing SEO across multiple properties with shared templates, overlapping content, and platform-level technical constraints requires a fundamentally different approach from single-site consulting.

You're launching in a new regulated market or jurisdiction

New license, new geo, new compliance requirements. Getting the technical and content foundations right before launch is significantly cheaper than fixing them after the fact.

Your current SEO activity isn't translating to revenue

Keyword rankings and traffic are vanity metrics if they're not moving the business. SEO consulting focused on revenue impact rather than ranking reports changes what gets prioritized and what gets cut.

You want an independent review of your current SEO strategy or agency

Before renewing a contract or committing budget, an independent senior strategist review can confirm whether the current approach is right for your vertical and growth stage.

You need senior SEO input without hiring a full-time head of SEO

A fractional consulting arrangement gives you experienced strategic oversight across technical SEO, content, and architecture without the cost or commitment of a permanent hire.
