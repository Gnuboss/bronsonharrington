Why 90 days and not an ongoing retainer?

The majority of foundational work - technical fixes, on-page optimisation, content calendar, and the start of gap fill - gets done in the first 90 days. A defined engagement with clear deliverables is more accountable than an open-ended retainer where scope creep and unclear outcomes are common.

What happens after 90 days?

You have a fully documented organic growth system and a clear picture of what's working. If you want to continue with ongoing growth, link acquisition, and content velocity, a monthly retainer is available from $3,500 per month. If you want to take it in-house, everything is documented and handed over cleanly.

Do you work with sites outside iGaming?

Yes. While iGaming and regulated verticals are the primary specialisation, the framework applies to any site where Google's quality scrutiny is meaningful. Health, legal, and financial services are all within scope.

What do you need from us to get started?

Google Search Console access, Ahrefs or similar tool access, and a clear brief on your licensed markets, platform, and current challenges. CMS access is needed for on-page implementation.

Can you work with our existing agency or in-house team?

Yes. The engagement can run alongside existing agency relationships or in-house teams with clear delineation of responsibilities agreed at the scoping stage.

Is the pricing fixed?

Yes. $17,500 for the full 90-day engagement. The price reflects what it takes to deliver the complete system properly. If the full engagement isn't the right fit right now, the free blueprint gives you the plan to execute yourself or with your team.

How many clients do you take on at once?

A limited number of engagements run simultaneously to ensure direct senior strategist attention on every client. If capacity is full, there is a waitlist.
