When You Need This

Signs Your Site Needs a Technical SEO Audit.

If any of these situations apply, a manual technical review is the right starting point.

Your organic traffic dropped and nobody can explain why

Rankings decline after a core update, a migration, or a platform change and the cause isn't obvious from surface-level reporting. A technical audit identifies what Google is actually seeing when it crawls your site.

You've recently migrated platforms, domains, or hosting

Migrations are the single most common source of unintentional SEO damage. Redirect chains, lost canonicals, broken hreflang, and crawl budget waste are standard post-migration issues that compound over time if not caught early.

Your Core Web Vitals are failing or borderline

Page experience signals are a confirmed ranking factor. LCP, CLS, and INP failures don't just hurt rankings, they directly impact conversion rates and user trust in regulated verticals where visitors already have high abandonment rates.

You're managing a white-label or multi-property portfolio

Template-level issues across a white-label stack can generate thousands of near-duplicate indexable URLs, waste crawl budget, and create canonical confusion that suppresses your highest-value pages across every property simultaneously.

Your site has grown significantly without a structural review

Sites that scale organically through content and features without periodic technical oversight accumulate debt that eventually shows up as indexation problems, ranking volatility, or crawl inefficiency.

You want to verify your current agency's technical work

An independent technical audit confirms whether existing SEO work is actually sound or whether there are issues being missed, misreported, or deprioritized that are costing you visibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Technical SEO Audits.

Answers to the questions most clients ask before getting started.

How is this different from running an automated audit tool?

Automated tools flag issues against a standard checklist. A manual audit interprets what those issues mean for your specific site, vertical, and growth stage, and tells you which ones actually matter for revenue.

What access do you need to conduct the audit?

Google Search Console, Ahrefs or similar backlink tool access, and ideally Google Analytics or GA4. CMS or hosting access is not required for the audit itself.

How long does a full technical audit take?

Typically five to seven business days from access being provided to the findings report and video walkthrough being delivered.

Do you fix the issues as well as identify them?

The audit identifies and prioritizes every issue with clear implementation guidance. Execution support is available as a separate engagement or as part of an ongoing consulting arrangement.

Can you audit a white-label platform where we don't control the server?

Yes. White-label audits specifically account for what can and can't be fixed at the platform level, and recommendations are scoped to what's actually implementable within your platform constraints.

How current does the audit stay after delivery?

A technical audit is a point-in-time review. For sites that change frequently or are scaling rapidly, a recurring technical oversight arrangement keeps the audit current on an ongoing basis.
