When You Need This

Signs Your Site Needs a Senior Recovery Strategist.

If your organic traffic has dropped and the cause isn't clear, recovery starts with the right diagnosis.

A core update wiped your rankings and they haven't recovered

Algorithm updates in regulated and YMYL verticals hit differently from standard niches. Google's quality rater guidelines and E-E-A-T signals play a larger role in these markets, and recovery requires understanding exactly which signals triggered the drop, not just what changed on the site.

A migration caused an organic traffic drop you can't explain

Domain migrations, platform switches, and hosting changes are the most common source of unintentional SEO damage. Broken redirects, lost canonicals, and crawl budget waste compound quickly and don't resolve themselves without intervention.

You received a manual action from Google

Manual penalties require a structured response: identifying the specific violation, remediating the issue, and submitting a reconsideration request that actually addresses Google's concerns. Generic fixes rarely result in successful reinstatement.

Your rankings have been declining gradually without an obvious cause

Gradual decline without a clear trigger is often a topical authority or E-E-A-T signal issue rather than a technical one. Identifying which signal has weakened and why requires a different diagnostic approach from a standard technical audit.

A competitor overtook you and you don't know how

Competitive displacement is rarely about a single factor. Understanding what shifted in the competitive landscape and which signals your competitors strengthened gives you a recovery roadmap that's actually targeted at closing the right gaps.

Previous recovery attempts haven't worked

If you've already tried to recover and traffic hasn't returned, the diagnosis was probably incomplete. A fresh senior strategist review often identifies the actual cause that previous attempts missed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About SEO Recovery.

Answers to the questions most clients ask before starting a recovery engagement.

How long does SEO recovery take?

Recovery timelines depend on the cause. Technical fixes can show results within weeks. Content and E-E-A-T signal recovery typically takes three to six months. Algorithm update recovery is tied to Google's next core update cycle.

Can you recover from a Google manual action?

Yes. Manual action recovery requires identifying the specific violation, remediating it completely, and submitting a structured reconsideration request. The success rate depends on how thoroughly the underlying issue is addressed.

How do you know what caused the traffic drop?

The diagnosis combines GSC data analysis, crawl review, content quality assessment, competitive landscape review, and correlation with known algorithm update dates to identify the most likely cause before any recovery work begins.

Do you guarantee recovery?

No. Any SEO strategist who guarantees recovery is not being honest about how Google's algorithm works. What I can guarantee is a thorough diagnosis, a recovery plan based on the actual cause, and transparent reporting on progress throughout the engagement.

Can you recover a site that's been penalized for years?

Yes, though the recovery timeline is typically longer for sites that have been suppressed for an extended period. The diagnostic process is the same regardless of how long the issue has been present.

What if we've already tried to recover and it didn't work?

A fresh diagnostic review often identifies what previous attempts missed. Share what's already been tried in the discovery call and the diagnosis will focus on what hasn't been addressed yet.
